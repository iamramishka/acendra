## Acceptance and implementation

Exercise provider-success evidence followed by internal persistence failure,
delayed retry, duplicate/conflicting delivery, and postcommit notification loss.
No partial settlement or paid entitlement may survive an aborted transaction.
After recovery, one bound settlement, event, grant, enrollment, audit and outbox
record must exist. Trusted entitlement verification must deny access after every
fault and allow access after recovery. Email delivery failure must not undo payment.

New SQL calls the actual merged checkout, initialization, finalization, entitlement,
notification claim and completion RPCs. Four transaction-local trigger faults target
event, grant, outbox and final audit persistence; assertions require the exact
injected SQLSTATE/message and verify rollback of every financial effect. Successful
same-event recovery, duplicate replay, changed-payload conflict, crashed lease
reclaim, stale token rejection and exhausted delivery are verified. Entire fixture,
triggers and queue changes roll back. Run this suite on a clean disposable database,
before independent-session suites that retain synthetic fixtures.

Two Node tests exercise notification delivery-response loss and acknowledgement
loss with the real worker and synthetic collaborators. Retries retain a stable
provider idempotency key. These tests do not prove external email exactly-once
delivery or real provider success; those require approved provider capabilities.
