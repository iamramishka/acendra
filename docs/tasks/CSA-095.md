## Implementation and contract

- One enrollment per user/course, with immutable beneficiary/course and first
  committed fulfillment timestamp. Safe own enrollment reads require eligible auth.
- Source grants carry `user_id`, `course_id`, `source_order_id`, `valid_from`,
  `valid_until`, `invalidated_at`, and `invalidation_reason`.
- Composite enrollment FK and order trigger prevent mismatched beneficiary,
  course, and method. One funding order creates at most one grant.
- Purchased grants require an order and lifetime `valid_until=null`. Admin grants
  require a different grantor and reason with no fabricated order. Source identity,
  initial validity and grant evidence are immutable. Invalidation cannot be undone.
- All financial/identity parents use RESTRICT. Grant rows have RLS and no direct
  student/service permissions; enrollment rows permit eligible own SELECT only.
- CSA-096 adds `settlement_id` and independently verified funding constraints.
  No grant/fulfillment/admin RPC is exposed by this schema.
