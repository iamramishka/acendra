## Additive read contract

`bank-transfer-read` accepts `{order_id, instructions: true}` in addition to its existing status/finance modes. Requires an authenticated, non-anonymous current verified owner session; finance MFA is not required for reading one's own unpaid order. Authenticated identity comes from the verified bearer, never from request fields. Unknown fields/mixed modes are rejected.

The service-only `csa276_read_bank_instructions(actor, session, aal, order)` returns the original checkout shape: order ID/reference, course ID/title snapshot, exact amount/currency, method/status/next action, and immutable bank instruction snapshot. Missing/other-owner/non-bank/non-payable orders share 404; invalid sessions fail closed. No storage paths, receipt evidence, private notes or other-student data are returned. No order, entitlement or financial mutation occurs.
